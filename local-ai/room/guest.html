Interview

Before you join. This interview runs on OpenMeet, on the interviewer's own computer. While you talk, that computer transcribes the conversation and looks at two things to help the interviewer: whether your eye movements look like reading, and whether your answers read like AI-written text. Nothing is sent to an online service and nothing is recorded; the interviewer keeps a written summary. These signals can be wrong, and they are a prompt for questions, not a judgement.
